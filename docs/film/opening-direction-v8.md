## Artwork prompt

Use case: ui-mockup. Create ONE exceptionally art-directed keyframe for a premium HappyHands software product film. Wide 16:9, pure clean near-white background. This is a frozen moment from sophisticated motion design, not a screenshot, not a presentation slide, not a flowchart. The feeling is lively, assured and exquisitely composed. Show a COMPLETE elegant fictional Northstar digital studio webpage becoming legible as its sections elegantly unfold out of a very compact link-entry control. The webpage is the dominant subject, not UI chrome: crisp editorial serif headline "Ideas into products.", large quiet sans-serif text, selected-work interface examples, studio/about section, contact form and visible footer. Entire page visible. Arrange it in a bold off-axis yet balanced cinematic composition, shallow three-dimensional depth, one selected page region gracefully peeling forward at larger scale, a small phone rendition moving into place. Use two understated motion trails and natural occlusion to imply the unfold/reveal; keep the central page tack sharp and readable. The enlarged fragment contains a refined compact inspector note labeled exactly "Review experience" and a small selection indicator, no fake score or success result. Do not surround everything with large boxes. No connecting lines, no wiring, no technical diagram, no symmetric three-column composition. Palette: warm white, graphite, a restrained living sage green accent. Typography deliberate and polished, English only. No URL strings, no localhost, no terminal, no file paths, no code, no loading spinner, no address text. No giant advertising headline outside the webpage, no giant numerals, no dense dashboard surrounding it. NO scenery, architecture, stairs, room, floor, physical paper, photography, people, plants, abstract blobs, glow or confetti. Preserve clean breathing room at the top left 240x90 pixels for a real logo to be overlaid later and leave bottom 8% quiet. No HappyHands logo or wordmark baked into image. Precise content, rich but restrained materials, excellent optical alignment, intentional asymmetry and visual rhythm. It must feel like a premium software launch film at the exact instant the interface assembles, NOT three flat screenshots pasted onto a slide.
